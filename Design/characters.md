# Characters

Cast and voice/register notes. Story context is in `story.md`, where
each character actually appears is in `levels.md`.

---

## The protagonist

Never named on-screen or in dialogue — everyone addresses him
indirectly or not at all, which is part of the erasure (`story.md`).
Fifteen years at the same job. Inner monologue is his voice throughout,
until it starts drifting to third person as he stops being registered
by the building.

## Halloran

His manager. Appears once, on Floor 10, and never again.

**Register:** flat, unhurried, not unkind. He isn't an obstacle and
isn't a villain — he's what fifteen years of doing this to people looks
like from the other side of the desk. He doesn't raise his voice, doesn't
apologize, doesn't gloat. He signs the resignation and means the one
line he says.

> *"Fifteen years. That's a long time. Good for you."*

**The phone call:** the only other thing he does. As the player leaves
his office, he's glimpsed through the glass dialing an extension,
talking too quietly to hear. No line, no subtitle. He is not the threat
below Floor 9 — this call is the only connection between him and it,
and it's deliberately left unexplained.

## Denise

Twenty-two years at the company. First appears on Floor 10.

**Register (Floor 10):** warm, genuinely fine, no regrets she'll admit
to. She isn't performing contentment — she's found a real, comfortable
groove in staying, which is worse than bitterness would be.

> *"You'll be back. Everyone comes back."*

**Register (Floor 6):** the same line returns, much later, in a much
worse context — see below. Keep her Floor 10 dialogue completely
unchanged; the horror is entirely in what the player now knows by the
time they hear it again, not in rewriting her.

**Floor 6 appearance:** found in one of the recharging pods, badly
diminished. Whether she's still fully a person or has been (partly)
replaced by whatever the pods actually do is never confirmed — write
her lines here ambiguous on that point, not leaning either way. She
still recognizes the player. She still means it kindly.

## The suit

Deliberately not a character in the usual sense — no name, no face, no
voice, no lines, ever. Do not write dialogue for it. Do not give it a
name even in comments/scripts (referring to it as "the suit" is
correct, not a placeholder).

**What it is, mechanically:** the roaming threat from Floor 7 downward
(patrols and re-files the player on contact — see `mechanics.md`).
Glimpsed twice earlier (Floor 9, Floor 8) as static, non-threatening
sightings before it starts actively hunting.

**What it is, narratively:** never resolved on purpose. It reads
equally as literally what the company sends after someone who won't
process out cleanly, or as something the building itself produces once
he stops being legible to it (see the "administrative erasure" framing
in `story.md`). Nothing in dialogue or the environment should tip this
either way.

## The new hire

Twenty-three, three weeks in. Appears at the party and again at a desk
on Floor 10.

**Register:** upbeat, a little oblivious, describing the job as
temporary in almost the same words the protagonist used fifteen years
ago. Doesn't remember meeting him between the two appearances. No
dialogue option exists to warn them, on purpose — see `story.md`.

## Marcus Vance

Never appears. Established entirely through his empty desk on Floor 9
— nameplate, boat photo, sales plaque (`levels.md`). No dialogue to
write; he's a password puzzle and a piece of set dressing, not a
character who needs a voice.
